Model card & limitationsengineering notes

Model architecture

Input layer: normalized control commands and scenario disturbance multipliers.

State layer: power, thermal, pressure, inventory, circulation, steam and output proxies.

Measurement layer: sensor bias, drift, noise and stuck-value faults are applied after state integration.

Numerical layer: fixed-step integration with bounded states, finite-value checks and recorded residual indicators.

Assumptions & exclusions

All values are dimensionless indices (0–100). There are no engineering units, plant setpoints, protection setpoints, equipment capacities, or real plant calibrations.

First-order response and normalized balances illustrate coupling only. The model is not a validated neutron-kinetics, thermal-hydraulics, fuel-performance, or safety-analysis code.

Passing internal software checks confirms numerical consistency of this toy model only; it does not validate physical fidelity.
